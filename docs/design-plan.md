# Design plan

Palette: white #ffffff, pale blue-gray #f2f5f7, border #ccd4dc, muted ink #526171, ink #1b2430, marker red #8c2639.

Use Source Sans 3 for body and headings, locally bundled under its OFL license. Body text is 18 px. Large headings establish the step; explanatory text stays under 65 characters per line.

Layout:

```
Lidcheck                            About
-----------------------------------------
Private by design
An eyelid photo. A clearer next step.
Explanation                         Eye diagram
Population choice
Take photo       Upload photo
-----------------------------------------
What happens to your photo
```

Review: use a single open canvas, rules between sections, and a pale instruction panel. Avoid repeated cards, shadows, decorative badges and gradients. Red appears only on the eye guide, ROI outline and signal marker. Results replace capture controls and receive keyboard focus. Study mode never renders a health result.
